# S-CAD Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

S-CAD is an offline viewer for DWG, SketchUp (SKP) and GLB files.

**Data we collect:** none. S-CAD does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

**Your files:** files you open are read and displayed on your device only. They are never uploaded to any server.

**Internet and accounts:** the app works fully offline. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

**Exports and sharing:** when you save a PNG or use "Share web copy", the file is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. What that app does with it is governed by that app.

**Children:** S-CAD does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
